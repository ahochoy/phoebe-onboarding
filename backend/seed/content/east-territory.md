# East Territory Notes

Working notes for the Sales East team at Larkspur Labs, covering the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic. These notes are updated by the team lead after each quarterly review. They are internal to the Sales organization.

## Territory overview

### Coverage

Sales East covers New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Massachusetts, Connecticut, and Maryland. The territory includes roughly 4,200 independent clinics that match our ideal customer profile. About 600 are current customers, and we are targeting 150 net new accounts this fiscal year.

## Segments and priorities

### Physical therapy clinics

Physical therapy is our strongest segment in the East. Clinics here have recurring appointments and feel missed visits keenly. Reference customers include a multi-clinic practice in Hartford, and a three-therapist clinic in Albany. Both have said that reminders cut no-shows by about a third.

### Dental practices

Dental is the second priority. The sales cycle is longer, usually 45 to 60 days, because owners tend to consult their office managers. Focus on the quality of the patient reminder experience and the insurance eligibility integration.

### Primary care

Primary care in the Northeast is dominated by large health systems, so we focus on independent practices. Expect questions about electronic record integration. We currently support two systems, and the roadmap includes a third in the second quarter.

## Regional notes

### Seasonality

Clinics are busiest in January and September. Avoid scheduling demos in the week between Christmas and New Year, and in the second half of August when owners are on holiday. Our best pipeline months are October and February.

### Competitors

In New York and New Jersey, the main competitor is a legacy scheduling product with a loyal installed base. In Pennsylvania and Maryland, many clinics still use paper or spreadsheets. Treat each competitor's customers respectfully, and never make claims about their products that you cannot back up.

### Events

We attend the regional physical therapy conference in Boston in March, and a dental society meeting in Philadelphia in May. Budget requests for events are due to Rafael one quarter in advance.
