Benefits at Larkspur Labs

We want you to be well, financially secure, and curious. This guide covers everything included in your Larkspur package as a full-time employee. Part-time employees working 20 or more hours a week receive the same plans at a prorated company contribution.

Enrolling in benefits

You must enroll within 30 days of your start date. Enrollment happens in the benefits portal, which you will get an invitation for on your first day. If you miss the window, your coverage options are limited until open enrollment each November or until a qualifying life event, such as marriage or the birth of a child. Coverage begins on the first of the month after your start date, and the company pays for the cost of your coverage described below.

  • Open the benefits portal from your first-day invitation email.
  • Choose a medical, dental, and vision plan, or waive any of them.
  • Add dependents, if any, with their date of birth.
  • Confirm your 401(k) contribution percentage.

Medical insurance

Larkspur offers two medical plans through Harbor Mutual Health, a fictional carrier. The Evergreen PPO has a $750 individual deductible and the widest network. The Cedar HSA plan has a $2,000 individual deductible and is paired with a health savings account that the company seeds with $600 each year. Larkspur covers 90% of the monthly premium for employees and 75% for dependents on either plan.

  • Primary care visits: $20 copay on Evergreen, deductible applies on Cedar.
  • Prescriptions: tiered copays, with generic drugs at $10.
  • Mental health visits: covered like primary care, with no limit on sessions.

Dental insurance

Dental coverage is provided through Bright Smile Dental, also fictional. Preventive care, including two cleanings per year, is covered at 100%. Basic procedures such as fillings are covered at 80%, and major procedures such as crowns at 50%. The annual maximum is $2,000 per person. Orthodontia is covered at 50% up to a $1,500 lifetime maximum.

Vision insurance

Vision coverage through ClearView Vision Care includes one eye exam per year with no copay. Every 12 months you get $200 toward frames or contact lenses. Corrective laser surgery is discounted by 15% at partner providers.

401(k) retirement plan

Larkspur offers a 401(k) with a 4% match. If you contribute 4% of your salary, the company contributes another 4%. Matching contributions vest immediately, so they are yours from day one. You can contribute up to the annual IRS limit and change your percentage at any time. New employees are enrolled automatically at 3%, and you can opt out or adjust in the portal during your first 30 days.

Time off and leave

Paid time off is 20 days per year, with sick leave separate. Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for every parent. See the Employee Handbook for the full leave policy, including carry-over rules.

Learning and wellbeing

  • Learning stipend: $1,200 per year for books, courses, and conferences.
  • Wellness allowance: $50 per month for a gym, class, or app of your choice.
  • Employee assistance program: free confidential counseling, eight sessions per issue, for you and your household.
  • Home office: up to $750 in your first month for equipment.
  • Commuter benefit: pre-tax transit and parking for the Portland and Chicago offices.

Who to ask

Questions about any of the above go to the People Operations team at benefits@larkspur-labs.example. The team holds open office hours every Tuesday at 14:00 Pacific, and new hires are encouraged to drop in during their first month.
