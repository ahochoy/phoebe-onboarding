Platform Project Manifest
Last reviewed: October 2026

This document lists the active projects on the Platform team this quarter, who leads them, and what done looks like. It is a living document that we update at each Monday planning session. If you want to join a project, talk to Jordan Lee or the project lead.

# Q4 2026 Projects

## Project Beacon 2.0
Lead: Dmitri Volkov
Status: In progress, 65 percent complete
Goal: Reduce the time from merge to production from 22 minutes to under 10 minutes by caching builds, running tests in parallel, and replacing the old canary step with progressive delivery.
Milestones: Build cache live in staging by November 20. Parallel test runner for all Go services by December 4. Progressive delivery pilot with the Reminders team in January.
Open question: how to handle database migrations in progressive rollouts. Design doc is in review.

## Trellis cluster upgrade to Kubernetes 1.34
Lead: Tomas Silva
Status: Planning
Goal: Upgrade all production clusters with no customer-visible downtime.
Milestones: Staging cluster upgraded by November 30. Production clusters upgraded one at a time through December 11, with a pause for the winter break.
Risks: Two deprecated APIs are still used by the Reminders service. Platform will help the owning team to migrate.

## Lantern alert cleanup
Lead: Anika Rao
Status: In progress
Goal: Cut non-actionable pages by half. We currently average 7 pages per on-call week outside working hours, and the target is under 3.
Milestones: Alert audit complete by November 13. Top ten noisy alerts fixed or deleted by November 27.
Good first task: This project has many small, well-defined tickets, and it is a great way to learn our systems. Mei Tanaka can point you to the starter tickets.

## Atlas ownership data
Lead: Mei Tanaka
Status: In progress
Goal: Every service in Atlas has a named owning team, an on-call rotation, and a link to its runbook. Today, 82 percent of services meet this bar, and we are aiming for 100 percent by the end of the quarter.

## Kit v3
Lead: To be assigned
Status: Proposed
Goal: Simplify the shared service libraries by merging the Go and Python configuration packages and dropping the legacy logging wrapper.
Notes: Waiting on capacity after Beacon 2.0. A new teammate could take part in the design discussion in December.

# How to propose a new project

Write a one-page proposal with the problem, who is affected, the proposed approach, and a rough size. Post it in the platform channel and bring it to Monday planning. Dmitri and Jordan review proposals together, and we try to answer within a week.
