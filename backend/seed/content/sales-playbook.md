# Sales Playbook

This playbook gives Larkspur Labs sales teams a shared way to find, qualify, and win customers. Our customers are small independent clinics, typically with 3 to 25 staff, who want scheduling and reminders that just work. It is written for account executives and development reps, and is owned by the Sales Operations lead.

## Who we sell to

### Ideal customer profile

Our best customers are independent physical therapy, dental, and primary care clinics with between 3 and 25 staff. They have no dedicated IT team, they lose revenue through missed appointments, and they are tired of juggling paper calendars and phone calls. Chains with more than 10 locations are outside our focus for now.

### Buyer personas

The practice owner cares about revenue, time saved, and patient satisfaction. The office manager cares about ease of use and how quickly the team can learn the tool. Always find out who is actually making the decision in the first call.

## The sales process

### Stages

1. Prospect: a clinic that matches the ideal customer profile and has not yet spoken to us.
2. Discovery: a 30 minute call to learn how they schedule appointments today and what hurts.
3. Demo: a tailored 30 minute walkthrough that shows the features that matter to them.
4. Trial: a 14 day trial with their real calendar, with a check-in on day 3 and day 10.
5. Proposal: a simple one-page quote with our standard pricing.
6. Closed won or closed lost: always record the reason in the CRM.

### Discovery questions

Ask how many appointments they book per week, how many no-shows they see, how reminders go out today, and what happens when someone calls after hours. Listen more than you talk, and summarize what you heard before moving on.

## Pricing and discounts

Standard pricing is $89 per provider per month, billed annually, or $99 billed monthly. Discounts up to 10% can be approved by the account executive. Discounts of 11 to 20% need the Sales Director. Do not discount more than 20% without a multi-year commitment, and never promise features that are not on the published roadmap.

## Handling objections

### "We already have a system"

Ask what they like about it and what they would change. Many clinics keep a system only because switching feels hard. Offer our free data import service and a named onboarding specialist.

### "It is too expensive"

Return to the cost of missed appointments. If a clinic loses just six appointments a month, at an average of $120 each, that is $720 in lost revenue, which is more than the cost of several providers.

## Working with the CRM

Log every call and email within a day. Update the stage as soon as it changes. Pipeline reviews happen every Monday morning, and only logged opportunities count toward forecasts.

## Quotas and compensation

Quota is set quarterly. Commission is paid monthly on closed won deals, with accelerators beyond 100% of quota. Ask your manager for the current plan document, because it changes each fiscal year.
